# AMUN SIGNAL product roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation (current)

- AMUN brand layer and Mission Control navigation shell
- Investigation domain models and canonical evidence hashing
- Upstream attribution, architecture guardrails, and baseline verification

## Phase 1 — Investigation core

- Case CRUD, activity timeline, assignments, saved views, and watchlists
- Entity resolution, aliasing, deduplication, and relationship graph
- Evidence capture with snapshots, SHA-256, provenance, confidence, and revisions
- Search across cases, entities, evidence, locations, and time

## Phase 2 — Intelligence fusion

- Signal-to-Case correlation and explainable scoring
- Geofences, rules, alerts, scheduled briefs, and replay
- AI Co-Analyst with evidence citations, hypotheses, contradiction checks, and research gaps
- Arabic RTL interface, Arabic NER, transliteration, and MENA source pack

## Phase 3 — Distribution and ecosystem

- Report Studio exports: PDF, Markdown, JSON, CSV, GeoJSON, STIX 2.1
- Connector/plugin SDK with permissions and health contracts
- Local-first/offline operation and self-hosted map packs
- Optional MISP and CASE/UCO interoperability

## Advanced candidates

- Collaborative investigation rooms with role-based access and audit trails
- Evidence redaction and disclosure packages
- Collection-plan builder with legal/ethical source constraints
- Source reliability calibration and analyst feedback loops
- Temporal graph diffing: what changed, when, and which evidence caused the change
